Dixie Fire Canopy Project

Restoring shade, beauty and tree canopy throughout communities affected by the Dixie Fire.

The Dixie Fire Canopy Project is working to bring trees back to neighborhoods and public spaces throughout our region. The project represents both environmental recovery and a long-term investment in the places residents call home.

Its work helps rebuild the natural character of our towns while creating cooler, greener and more welcoming spaces for future generations.
